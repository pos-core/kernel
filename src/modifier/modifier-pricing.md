# Modifier Pricing

Modifier pricing is the calculated financial result of a hydrated modifier configuration.

A priced configuration contains an ordered contribution for each priced selected choice and the sum of those contributions. Each contribution preserves its selected choice, resolved label, quantity, selection source, flat or invariant-rate definition, applied factors, and resulting money amount.

This total covers modifier contributions. A catalog item adds it to the selected variant's invariant price to obtain the configured item total.
